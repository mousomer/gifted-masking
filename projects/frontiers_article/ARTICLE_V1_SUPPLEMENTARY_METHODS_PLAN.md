# Article V1 — Supplementary Methods Plan

**Stage 3 routing artifact.** This document defines the owned scientific
Supplementary Methods source for the manuscript draft. It does not replace the
formal Methods authority or reproduce implementation/governance records.

## Supplementary Methods contents

1. Complete notation and reference-scale table, parameter domains, and the
   reward-scale/temperature identity.
2. Learner reward transform, stable softmax implementation, initialization,
   time indexing, and final-window definition.
3. Complete M0/M1 reward equations, loss-weighted sensitivity interpretation,
   affine disclosure derivation, and endpoint theorem.
4. M2 reward table, transition kernels, Q-learning update, value-iteration
   formulation, policy enumeration, aggregate Neutral classifier, visit
   adequacy, and sensitivity/harsh-sentinel definitions.
5. M3 reward classes, action indexing, flat/hierarchical/global/fixed-known
   update mechanics, K-invariance proof, and comparison-cost derivation.
6. Exact E01–E05 configurations, grids, common-random-number rules, master
   seed hierarchy, replication units, estimands, tie rules, and uncertainty
   procedures.
7. E05 PAVA weighting, bootstrap resampling, crossing inversion, endpoint
   anchors, and hierarchical left-censoring procedure.
8. Expanded sensitivity/secondary-result tables and an analytic-results table.
