## Meaning-preservation checks

1. A parameter-cell fraction is not a human prevalence estimate.
2. A 5,000-step five-sentinel result is not a long-horizon full-grid result.
3. A successful encounter is an experienced outcome, not proof of full knowledge.
4. A modal-population crossing is not a 50%-masking crossing.
5. Regret and intensity linked by an identity are not independent evidence.
6. Separate perturbations are not a factorial test; changing gamma changes
   the objective benchmark as well as the learner's target.
7. Historical artifact verification does not recover missing historical code.
8. No E06/M4 numerical result or unexecuted sensitivity is added.
