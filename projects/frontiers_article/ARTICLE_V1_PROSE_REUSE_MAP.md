**Status:** extraction routing complete

**Rule:** reuse is permitted only after reconciliation against the evidence
freeze, evidence matrix, consistency audit, analytic authority, and Formal
Methods. Historical fluency never outranks current scientific authority.

## Reuse action vocabulary

- **REUSE NEARLY DIRECTLY** — scientifically current and near target length.
- **COMPRESS** — scientifically usable but too long or repetitive.
- **MERGE WITH OTHER SOURCE** — combine dispersed correct fragments.
- **REWRITE FOR CURRENT AUTHORITY** — rhetorical structure may help, but claims or scope
  require rebuilding from current authority.
- **MOVE TO SUPPLEMENT** — accurate detail is outside the main-text budget.
- **SOURCE FOR FACTS ONLY** — use the authority, not its prose form.
- **DISCARD** — stale, excluded, or scientifically unsafe.
- **NEW WRITING REQUIRED** — no coherent passage currently performs the required job.
