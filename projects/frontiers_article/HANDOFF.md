## 6. Current scientific position

### E01 — generic information restriction baseline

An initial rejection reduced later authentic sampling. Its normalized pseudo-regret contrast is algebraically the same action-frequency information expressed in objective-loss units, not an independent confirmation. No increase in final-window masking was detected. Forced authentic exploration and counterfactual feedback reduced model masking and normalized pseudo-regret after rejection.

### E02 — graded independent actions

Five independent graded actions preserved more nonzero disclosure than binary endpoints after rejection, while full-authentic endpoint selection and modal-optimum discovery were worse. In the relevant affine cells, normalized regret is exactly mean endpoint distance `1-d`; it is not independent welfare evidence. The main scientific caveat is the independent-arm representation.

### E03 — persistent-state environment

The exact full-information M2 comparator is strongly verified: independent deterministic stationary-policy enumeration matched the value-iteration envelope over all 357 frozen cells to maximum error `8.99e-10`. The publication-facing classifier is **aggregate final-window Neutral-state behavior**, not individual Q-order policy prevalence. In 243/357 tested cells that aggregate behavior favored masking while the full-information DP favored authenticity. This is tested-grid coverage, not prevalence and not an information-matched failure claim.

### E04 — scarce receptivity under representation

Combined flat/hierarchical, known-good, global-mask, randomized-identity, two-good, and proportional-good controls support a bounded conclusion: **representation-dependent learning burden under scarce receptivity**. They do not identify K alone, scarcity alone, duplicate-mask normalization alone, or literal human movement among peer groups as the unique cause.

### E05 — descriptive learned/objective separation; mechanism unresolved

The flat `K=4` across-child modal-good-authentic response crosses `0.5` near `bf=0.312852` with master-bootstrap interval `[0.307836, 0.316918]`, before the exact environmental crossover `8/17 ≈ 0.470588`. The objective authenticity advantage at the point crossing is about `0.134076`.

**Correction:** the binary softmax theorem `P(A)=.5 iff Q_A=Q_B` does not apply to this E05 statistic. E05 is an eight-action, across-child final-window modal-arm fraction. Fixed temperature and action-set normalization therefore remain possible contributors to the displacement. The current experiment does not identify the cause.
