# Methods followed by results lecture

Sixteen slides, 900 seconds of planned narration, 1545 spoken words.

- Slides 1–3 preserve the earlier opening and its assets, with revised timing.
- Slides 4–8 cover M0, M1, M2, M3 and M4 methods only.
- Slides 9–13 report E01, E02, E03, E04 and E05 in model order.
- Slide 14 explicitly states that M4/E06 formal results remain deferred.
- Slides 15–16 discuss mechanisms and empirical measurements.

Methods contain reward definitions, action/state diagrams and learner memory,
not result charts or estimated effects. Result slides contain no repeated model
setup diagrams. M2 has three social states and two actions; M3 uses context–
behaviour actions; M4 carries continuous environmental receptivity but its
learner stores two action-only values. No scientific semantics changed.
