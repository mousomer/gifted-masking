## 2. Frozen scientific identity

Article V1 is an existence-and-structure computational modelling paper. Its
central claim is that learned concealment or reduced authenticity need not
imply that concealment is objectively advantageous within the specified model.

The model progression is `M0 -> M1 -> M2 -> M3`. E03, E04, and E05 carry most
of the paper's argumentative weight; E01 and E02 are supporting minimal and
action-representation results. M4/E06 is excluded from V1 evidence.

## 3. Binding claim boundaries

- E01 shows reduced later authentic sampling, not persistent final masking;
  sampling and normalized regret are linked transformations.
- E02 changes disclosure behaviour but does not create an interior static
  optimum or independent welfare evidence.
- E03 `243/357` is tested-grid coverage, not prevalence, natural phase-space
  volume, or individual-policy frequency; its comparator is full-information.
- E04 is conditional on the reference heterogeneous-context design and the
  conjunction of scarcity, discovery, finite sampling, and representation.
- E05 is a descriptive learned/objective separation. The binary-softmax
  proposition is not mechanism proof, and the hierarchical crossing is
  left-censored in range.
- Baseline `lambda=1` is loss-neutral.
- No parameter provenance is scientific justification or human calibration.
