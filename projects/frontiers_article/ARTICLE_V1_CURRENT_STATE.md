## 4. E01 — restricted feedback

### Frozen findings

- rejected-minus-accepted post-initial authentic-action rate: about `-0.025837`;
- corresponding normalized pseudo-regret contrast: about `+0.025824`;
- final-window masking contrast: `-0.000343`, interval `[-0.011605, 0.010918]`;
- forced authentic exploration and counterfactual feedback reduce final masking and normalized pseudo-regret after rejection;
- higher negative-reward weighting has a large general masking effect, but no detected interaction showing that it specifically amplifies one controlled initial rejection.

### Analytic qualification

In the relevant authenticity-optimal binary cells,

`normalized pseudo-regret = number of mask actions / T`.

Because the first action is forced authentic, the sampling and regret contrasts are exactly linked by the factor `(T-1)/T`. They are not independent evidence.

### Maximum interpretation

Restricted feedback changed later sampling in the tested model. No increase in persistent final-window masking after one rejection was detected.

## 5. E02 — graded independent disclosure

### Frozen findings

- graded `d>0` sampling minus binary full-authentic resampling: `+0.255040`;
- graded-minus-binary full-authentic final-window rate: `-0.328692`;
- graded-minus-binary optimal-endpoint modal probability: `-0.221740`;
- binary-minus-graded normalized pseudo-regret: `-0.029761` (graded worse).

### Analytic qualification

M1 expected reward is affine in disclosure and the strict static optimum is always an endpoint except at exact indifference. In the relevant authenticity-optimal cells,

`normalized regret(d) = 1-d`.

Thus the E02 regret result is mean objective endpoint distance, not an independent welfare mechanism.

### Maximum interpretation

Independent graded arms changed the exposure distribution of exploration but did not improve full-authentic endpoint discovery or objective endpoint distance.

## 6. E03 — persistent social state

Under the frozen default learner, **aggregate final-window Neutral-state action counts** favored masking while the exact full-information dynamic optimum favored authenticity in `243/357` tested cells. Seven low-`p`, `eta=0` cells were visit-inconclusive; the remaining classification geometry was stable under the frozen selected streams.

These selected points are not a general monotonicity theorem.

### Maximum interpretation
